# Electrical review — incomplete qualification

Reviewed 2026-09-05. The pinned KiCad schematic is the architectural reference. Its connections are not assumed correct merely because the netlist exports successfully. The physical pin mappings below and in [PIN-MAPPING.md](PIN-MAPPING.md) are checked against manufacturer material; operating limits, timing, layout and hardware compatibility still have the open items stated here. No fabrication approval is implied.

## Power and sequencing

- **U5/U13, TI TPS63802DLAR, C2845237:** pins 1 EN, 2 MODE, 3 AGND, 4 FB, 5 PG, 6 VOUT, 7 L2, 8 power GND, 9 L1, 10 VIN. Original MODE remains grounded. Both 0.47 µH inductors remain between L1/L2. With 0.5 V feedback, U5's 820 kΩ/91 kΩ divider gives 5.005 V and U13's 510 kΩ/91 kΩ gives 3.302 V, before tolerances. The 1.3–5.5 V operating input and >1.8 V startup requirement cover two AA cells above the reference undervoltage threshold. Buck/boost current, capacitor DC-bias derating and transient limits still need a load budget. [TI TPS63802](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63802.pdf).
- **U3/U10/U17, TI TPS3840DL20DBVR, C2066745:** pins 1 active-low open-drain RESET, 2 VDD, 3 GND, 4 MR, 5 CT. CT and the two unused MR pins remain open as the datasheet permits. The DL20 option has a nominal 2.0 V threshold. The first supervisor drives the power enable sequence; the other two provide battery indication logic. Pull-up paths remain in the reference nets. [TI TPS3840](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3840.pdf).
- **U11/U12/U18, TI TPS22917DBVR, C2681320:** pins 1 VIN, 2 GND, 3 ON, 4 CT, 5 QOD, 6 VOUT. The reference's CT capacitors connect to VIN, consistent with TI's circuit; CT must not be blindly moved to GND. U12 has open CT, which is allowed. QOD remains connected to VOUT. The 1–5.5 V rating covers the nominal rails; worst-case 5 V overshoot remains unmeasured. [TI TPS22917](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22917.pdf).
- **U4/U8, TI TPS74525PQWDRVRQ1, C2865628:** selected for fixed 2.5 V, 500 mA capability, EN and open-drain PG. Pin remapping, thermal-pad ground and recommended NC ground are explicit. Selected PG timing is approximately 150 µs nominal, not a guarantee of the original NCV8164 timing. Output discharge, 53 µV RMS typical noise, dropout and current limit differ. VDD2/VAUD current and audio performance require qualification. [TI TPS745-Q1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps745-q1.pdf).
- **L1/L2, DFE252012P-R47M=P2, C237552:** retained 0.47 µH function with a catalog 4 A-rated part. Saturation under peak buck/boost current and the enlarged placement's high-frequency loops need review. Input/output capacitors must be brought into tight loops before routing is accepted.
- **F1, 0466002.NRHF, C3105:** 2 A fast fuse, changed to 1206. The original 0805 2 A part could not be imported. Current rating alone does not prove equivalent clearing time or I²t. **PTC1**, 0805L075SLYR, C207023, retains its 0.75 A hold-current function. Coordination with the fuse and connectors is open.

## Audio and indicators

- **U7:** reference TLV9364 requires at least 4.5 V, but VAUD is 2.5 V. TI TLV9064IPWR (C779410), rated 1.8–5.5 V, uses the same 14-pin quad layout. Pin 4 → VAUD, pin 11 → AGND. All four original output/inverting-input feedback pairs remain intact: 1/2, 7/6, 8/9, 14/13. Filter passives remain; noise, stability and output drive are not bench-verified. [TLV936x](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9361.pdf), [TLV9064](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9064.pdf).
- **U6, TI LM4853MM/NOPB, C2872995:** 1 LIN, 2 shutdown, 3 headphone detect, 4 AGND, 5 RIN, 6 ROUT, 7 bypass, 8 BTL+, 9 VAUD, 10 LOUT/BTL−. At 2.5 V −1.5%, 2.4625 V remains above the 2.4 V minimum. The speaker is floating between the BTL outputs; stereo headphone coupling, detection and shutdown circuitry are retained. Speaker load/output-power and capacitor selection still need qualification. [TI LM4853](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm4853.pdf).
- **U9, TI SN74LVC2G34DBVR, C347589:** noninverting dual buffer, pins 1 input 1, 2 AGND, 3 input 2, 4 output 2, 5 VAUD, 6 output 1. Its 1.65–5.5 V supply range includes VAUD and its inputs tolerate the upstream digital level. The upstream linked G14 document is for an inverting device and is not the selected part. [TI SN74LVC2G34](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g34.pdf).
- **U14, MIC1557YM5-TR, C144169:** the source symbol says MIC1553 but the upstream BOM says MIC1557. Selection follows the BOM after checking pins 1 timing, 2 GND, 3 chip select, 4 supply, 5 output. VOUT5 covers its 2.7–18 V supply range. Oscillation and battery flash thresholds need hardware tests. [Microchip MIC1555/1557](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/MIC1555-57-IttyBitty-RC-Timer-Oscillator-DS20005730B.pdf).
- **Discrete switches:** NDC7002N (C114648): G1/S1/D1 = 1/2/6 and G2/S2/D2 = 3/5/4. 2N7002LT1G (C16338): G/S/D = 1/2/3. MMBT3904LT1G (C81464) and MMBT3906LT1G (C53444): B/E/C = 1/2/3. Original assignments are retained. MOSFET conduction at low gate voltage needs margin checks; threshold voltage is not a guaranteed low on-resistance. [onsemi NDC7002N](https://www.onsemi.com/download/data-sheet/pdf/ndc7002n-d.pdf), [2N7002L](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/2n7002l-d.pdf), [MMBT3904L](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mmbt3904l-d.pdf), [MMBT3906L](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mmbt3906l-d.pdf).

## CPU, memory, display and controls

- **U1 CPU AGB:** all 128 real pins and interface nets remain. No verified JLCPCB listing or public Nintendo electrical datasheet was found. Its original PCB package remains unresolved under the no-custom-footprint rule. CPU voltage limits, oscillator drive, memory timing and compatibility cannot be independently signed off from the reference alone.
- **U2 SRAM:** CY62146EV30LL-45ZSXI (C466866) permits 2.2–3.6 V; both VDD2 supply pins and both ground pins are connected. A 2.5 V ±1.5% rail is inside that range. The earlier IS62WV12816BLL candidate was rejected because its 2.5 V minimum exceeds the worst-case low rail. Address/data/byte enables are independently checked by the audit. The extra address bit is grounded through populated JP2; the old BYTE option is unsupported. The 200 µs power-up access restriction and CPU read/write timing remain open. [Infineon SRAM](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/10/49/infineon-cy62146ev30-mobl-4-mbit-256k-x-16-static-ram-datasheet-en.pdf).
- **U15/U16 hotkeys:** SN74LVC1G332DBVR (C43368), OR inputs 1/3/6, output 4, GND 2, VDD3 5. SN74HC02PWR (C484750), GND 7, VDD3 14; LRST feeds 3/6/8/11 and outputs remain 1/4/10/13. Verify HC input thresholds against the final measured VDD3 and button logic. [TI OR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g332.pdf), [TI NOR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc02.pdf).
- **X1 crystal:** C718663 is the correct 4.194304 MHz frequency in a larger HC-49S-SMD package, with 20 pF specified load. The original 27 pF/33 pF pair contributes 14.85 pF in series before stray/CPU capacitance. This is not enough information to prove 20 pF load matching, drive-level safety or startup margin; those are blockers. Its two terminals are passive and neither is a ground pin. [Manufacturer's 49SSMD series document, distributor mirror](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/a89de633c00fc814f650f75145597b77.pdf?productCode=C718668).
- **Display:** every original P2 signal and unused pin remains represented. Z57/Z58 are populated as 100 pF from the original “100p or 0ohm” option; choosing the correct screen/bridge variant is still required. There is no qualified LCD assembly selection, FFC mating/contact-side check, or independent Nintendo LCD electrical specification.
- **Buttons, switch, volume, headphone connector:** pin assignments and mechanical substitutions are detailed in PIN-MAPPING.md. The Alps tactile switch forbids copper beneath portions of its body; these keepouts must be implemented and verified before routing, beyond the imported courtyard.

## Passive values and remaining limits

The generated passive catalog preserves every fixed R/C value; Z57/Z58 explicitly choose 100 pF. Small signal capacitors use C0G where selected; bypass/bulk capacitors use X7R/X5R. CP1/CP2/CP3 are implemented as nonpolar 100 µF MLCCs, so polarity is no longer a placement constraint, but effective capacitance, leakage, microphonics and low-ESR stability are not proven equivalent to the reference. All passives and connectors retain real JLCPCB identifiers in the compiled BOM. Supplier stock snapshots are not an assembly reservation.

Independent verification of every connection is **not complete**. In particular, proprietary CPU/display specifications, complete load and timing budgets, analog measurements, protection coordination, detailed land-pattern qualification and the final enclosure/cables remain open. The connection audit establishes faithful translation of the documented reference plus explicit changes; it cannot replace those checks.
